Course objectives

Draft for instructor approval. By the end of this course you will be able to:

  1. S1. Explain the role of statistics in psychology and society, including why statistical reasoning guards against biased intuition.
  2. S2. Classify variables by scale of measurement and explain how a study's design determines which statistics are appropriate.
  3. S3. Summarize data with appropriate descriptive statistics and graphs, and recognize misleading displays.
  4. S4. Use probability, the normal distribution, and sampling distributions to reason about samples and populations.
  5. S5. Choose, conduct, and interpret hypothesis tests (including t-tests, chi-square, correlation, regression, and ANOVA) with effect sizes and assumptions.
  6. S6. Evaluate the reliability and validity of a measure.
  7. S7. Report and interpret statistical results accurately in clear, discipline-appropriate language.
  8. S8. Use SPSS to enter, clean, analyze, and display data accurately.

How the work in this course connects to the objectives

Each graded assignment practices the objectives marked Yes. Weekly Concept Reviews in Methods Market practice the reading-based objectives listed for each week.
