Course objectives

By the end of this course you will be able to:

  1. R1. Explain the scientific approach and distinguish the major research designs (experimental, quasi-experimental, non-experimental, survey, qualitative, archival, single-subject).
  2. R2. Locate, evaluate, and synthesize peer-reviewed literature on a topic.
  3. R3. Formulate a researchable question and hypothesis grounded in the literature.
  4. R4. Select and justify a methodology path and describe participants, procedures, and measures in a methods section.
  5. R5. Evaluate measurement quality, including operational definitions, reliability, and validity.
  6. R6. Apply ethical principles and IRB processes to a human-subjects study.
  7. R7. Interpret descriptive and inferential statistics reported in research.
  8. R8. Write and format research documents in APA 7th edition style.
  9. R9. Collaborate with a group and respond professionally to reviewer feedback.

Each graded assignment practices the objectives marked Yes. Weekly Concept Reviews in Methods Market practice the reading-based objectives listed for each week.
