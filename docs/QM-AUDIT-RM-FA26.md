## Headline findings (most valuable first)

1. **Learning objectives are the biggest gap (GS 2, 15 of the Essential points).** The syllabus has two course "goals" and two "specific goals", written partly as "to help fulfil requirements for students majoring in psychology" (not an outcome). There are **no module/week objectives**, none on assignment pages, and no map from objective to activity to assessment. The Canvas Course Overview page is still the **unedited template** ("[Objective 1] [Objective 2]"). The shared outcomes tree (`workspace/outcomes/courses/psyc-4223.json`) already has draft CLOs that fix this once Chris approves them.
2. **Template placeholders are still published (GS 1, 8).** Active pages with leftover template text: `course-overview`, `home-page-one`, `home-page-two` ("Type Your Course Title Here", with the vendor licence notice), `week-1-overview-and-to-do-list`, `week-1-presentation`, `week-1-reflection-and-looking-ahead`, `week-1-supplementary-resources`. Also published: `contact-me` and `contact-me-2` (duplicates), `written-proposal-example` (joke "alien" example), `final-paper-instructions` (a bare Zoom link and passcode, **a credential sitting on a student-visible page**), `human-research-training` (says "upload on **blackboard**"), `class-recording-irb-from-tandy-keenan` (2023-era). Unpublish or rewrite all of them.
3. **Canvas, syllabus and MM disagree.** Canvas and syllabus still list **"Phase 3: Elevator Speech/Worksheet"** and **"Phase 4 Worksheet"** both due Oct 30; MM retired Elevator Speech, renamed Phase 4 to Method Choice (week of Oct 12), and added a Measures Draft (Oct 23) that has no Canvas assignment. MM schedules **IRB first draft Oct 30**; Canvas and syllabus say **Nov 6**. Students will see two different calendars (QM 3.2 "consistent throughout", 1.1, 8.1).
4. **Assessment criteria are thin (GS 3).** Three Canvas assignments have no instructions at all (Literature Review Draft 1, IRB First Draft, Roll Call), IRB Final has one sentence. Only two rubrics exist in the export (Literature Review, Formal Proposal) and neither is attached to an assignment in the export (verify in Canvas). Article Review (200), Phase 4 (200), Methods (250) and IRB Status Update (400) have prose but no criteria with point levels.
5. **Point weights do not follow the stated goals.** Of 2,530 points: Part 3 (HRT certificates + IRB) 43.5%; HRT certificate uploads alone 500 pts (19.8%); IRB Status Update 400 (15.8%, a status report); the Methods Section, the core of Goal 2, 250 (9.9%); weekly Concept Reviews 5.1%. Assignment group weights are all 0.0 in the export (points-based grading, which is fine, but the syllabus calls the groups "weighted"-looking subtotals). Chris decides; see PEDAGOGY-REVIEW.md.
6. **Learner support is half there (GS 7).** Disability services and technical support (Canvas help desk page) are present. Missing: academic support (tutoring/writing center/library), student services, and a privacy statement for MM (which collects student keys and practice data) and Canvas tools.
7. **Strengths worth keeping:** a clear "Getting Started on Methods Market" page; a published weekly schedule with every due date and point value; trust-based late policy stated in syllabus and Canvas page; AI policy; accessibility/ADA statement; full 13-chapter reading map; free open textbook; MM already has 87 measurable Concept Review objectives.
