Module 8 software practice datasets
All records are synthetic teaching examples, not real people or research findings.
No student must collect or simulate data. Use the exact file and columns named in each task. Each CSV row is one observation; do not enter cell counts as observations. ID is an identifier.
The Learn walkthroughs retain their named class files; these files are for the separately listed practice exercises.

module8_learning.csv: 90 synthetic observations: groups, teaching methods, correlation, paired and repeated measures.
module8_survey.csv: 300 synthetic survey observations: Major and StressLevel.
module8_contingency.csv: 100 synthetic independent observations: Group and Outcome.
module8_small_counts.csv: 16 synthetic observations for Fisher exact test.
module8_paired_categories.csv: 100 synthetic paired categorical observations.
module8_suits.csv: 200 synthetic suit selections for goodness-of-fit.
module8_skewed.csv: 40 synthetic observations for rank-based practice.

module8_learning.csv: Score is the independent-group outcome; MethodScore is the teaching-method outcome. Before/After and Speech/Conceptual/Syntax are paired within each ID. StudyHours, ExamScore and SleepHours are continuous. Group and Method are nominal.
module8_survey.csv: Major and StressLevel are nominal for chi-square.
