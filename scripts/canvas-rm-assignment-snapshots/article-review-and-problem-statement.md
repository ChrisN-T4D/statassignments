## Requirements

Complete at least **6 peer-reviewed, original research article reviews** (6–8 on the template). For each article include:

- APA reference
- The authors' research questions
- Participants, methodology, results
- Strengths, weaknesses, and connection to your proposal

Write in complete sentences; summarize, do not copy from the article.

After all article cards, draft your **Problem Statement** (what we know, gap, what we want to know) and preliminary research question(s) and hypothesis(es).

Attach a PDF for every article in Canvas. Click **Start guided tour** in Study Plan for field-by-field guidance.
