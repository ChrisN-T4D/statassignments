Required Tools/Actions




IV is Manipulated
(You control the cause) AND
DV is Numeric/Scores (Quantifiable Effect)

	

Experimental / Quasi-Experiment Design
(Correlation or Causation study)

	

You need to compare groups (e.g., High Sleep vs. Low Sleep). Data must be numerical for analysis.

	

Random assignment sheets,
Pre/Post-test forms,
LMS Gradebook access, Lab protocols.




IV is Observed
(No manipulation) AND
DV is Numeric/Scores *(Correlation study)

	

Survey Study
(Observational research)

	

You are measuring a relationship between existing habits and outcomes without changing the environment.

	

Questionnaires, Scales,
Databases (e.g., GPA records),
Standardized inventories.




IV or DV is Subjective
(Meaning-based) OR
Goal: Understand Experience/Depth.

	

Qualitative / Mixed Methods Design
(Thematic Analysis)

	

You need to explain "Why" variables interact, not just measure the interaction itself with numbers. Focus on nuance/narrative first (or supplement).

	

Interview transcripts,
Focus group guides,
Journals/Diaries.
Audit logs for behavior observation.
