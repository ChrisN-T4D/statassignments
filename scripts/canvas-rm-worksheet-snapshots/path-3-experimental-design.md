Path 3 - Experimental Design
Path 3: Experimental Design

Best For: Establishing causality, manipulation of variables.
Software: SPSS or Jamovi (Required for Analysis)
Constraint: Focus on 2 to 3 Core Variables.

Phase 1: Protocol & Deep Design

Manipulation: Define IV operationalization (Treatment Manual).
Control: Define Control Condition (Placebo/Waitlist).
Scripts: Create introduction/instruction/debriefing scripts.
Pilot: Conduct pilot test (N=3−5) to check feasibility.
Deliverable: Pilot Test Report & Experimental Protocol.

Phase 2: Ethics & IRB

Consent: Include group assignment details and debriefing for deception.
Deliverable: IRB Approval Letter.

Phase 3: Sampling & Collection

Assignment: Randomization Log (Coin flip or RNG).
Deliverable: Randomization Log & Raw Data.

Phase 4: Analysis (SPSS/Jamovi)

Tests: t-test, ANOVA, or Regression.
Assumptions: Check Normality/Homogeneity.
Deliverable: Statistical Output PDF.
