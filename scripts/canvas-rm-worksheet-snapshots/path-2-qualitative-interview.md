Path 2: Qualitative Interview Research

Best For: Exploring experiences, motivations, themes.
Software: NVivo or Manual Coding (Transcription) + SPSS/Jamovi (for Data Management only).
Constraint: Focus on 2 to 3 Core Research Questions/Themes.

Phase 1: Protocol Design

Guide: Create a Semi-Structured Interview Guide (10–15 open-ended questions).
Themes: Map questions to 2–3 Core Themes.
Deliverable: Interview Protocol.

Phase 2: Ethics & IRB

Consent: Must specify recording method (Audio/Video) and anonymity.
Video: Specific addendum for video consent.
Deliverable: IRB Approval Letter.

Phase 3: Sampling & Collection

Method: Purposive or Snowball Sampling (N=15–30N = 15–30N=15–30).
Recording: Record audio/video; Transcribe accurately.
Deliverable: Transcripts (Anonymized).

Phase 4: Analysis (Coding)

Code: Line-by-line coding or thematic analysis.
Output: Theme mapping with representative quotes.
Deliverable: Qualitative Analysis Report.
