Fractions, Decimals, and Percentages

Fractions, decimals, and percentages are three ways to write the same number. Statistics moves between them constantly.

1/20.5050%
3/40.7575%

Converting

  • Fraction to decimal: divide the top by the bottom. 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75.
  • Decimal to percentage: multiply by 100 (move the point two places right). 0.75 → 75%.
  • Percentage to decimal: divide by 100. 5% → 0.05.
  • Decimal to fraction: 0.75 is 75 hundredths, 75/100, which reduces to 3/4 by dividing top and bottom by 25.

A percentage of a quantity

To find 20% of 150 students, convert to a decimal and multiply: 0.20 × 150 = 30.

Proportions and probabilities

Later in the course, a proportion is a decimal between 0 and 1, and a probability is also written between 0 and 1. A probability of .05 means 5%, or 1 in 20.

Rounding

Keep extra decimal places while you calculate and round only at the end. In this course, report most statistics to two decimal places unless told otherwise. Software may show more digits than you need.

Key Takeaways

  • A fraction, a decimal, and a percentage can all describe the same value.
  • Multiply by 100 to go from a decimal to a percentage, and divide by 100 to go back.
  • Proportions and probabilities are written as decimals between 0 and 1.
  • Round only at the last step.
